Lekhika is written by one person: no company behind it, no marketing budget. If it earns a place in your writing life, these are the ways to keep it going.

Send a gift — PayPal
https://paypal.me/VidyutG
A one-off gift, entirely optional: the software is free and open source whether you send one or not.

My books — Abrahamha's Strays
https://vidyut.net/category/books/
The science fiction I write, set in a world of dorms, domes and immortal sisters. Buying or reviewing them is the other way this work gets paid for; both are on Kindle Unlimited, so reading them costs nothing extra if you subscribe.

The newsletter — subscribe
https://vidyut.net/newsletter-signup/
Occasional notes on the writing, the books and this software: what changed and what is coming.

Tell someone. There is no marketing budget here, so a recommendation goes further than anything else on this page: the software to a writer who would use it, and the books to someone who reads this kind of science fiction.

Report a bug or ask for a feature
https://github.com/Vidyut/lekhika/issues/new
Problems and requests collect in one place, so they can be fixed and built. Copy the system details from the Help page first, then say what you did, what happened, and what you expected.
