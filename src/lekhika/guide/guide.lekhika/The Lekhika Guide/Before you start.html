Lekhika is a free, open-source writing app for books and long-form projects. It helps you focus on writing, keep your manuscript organised, and produce exports you can share or publish — without locking your work inside the app.

[Screenshot: Lekhika window showing the binder, editor and inspector]

Why writers choose Lekhika

  • Free and open source. No account, no subscription, no lock-in.
  • Automatic updates (when you want them). Choose how updates are checked and installed in Preferences — or turn checks off entirely.
  • Easy exports, highly customisable. Export to DOCX, EPUB, PDF, HTML or plain text with simple defaults, or use templates, page setup and placeholders to control layout and formatting — including exports suitable for uploading to platforms such as Amazon KDP.
  • Files as truth. Your project lives as ordinary files and folders on disk, with order and metadata preserved, so your work stays accessible and under your control.
  • Better paste for book formatting. Control what formatting is kept when you paste, so bringing text in does not mangle your manuscript.
  • Dictionary that includes in-world terms. Spell-check uses your system dictionary plus a project word list, and project scan helps you add names and other project-specific terms in batches.
  • Read aloud (TTS). Hear your prose to catch rhythm, clarity and mistakes.
  • AI assistance, on your terms. Optional local or hosted AI for continue, proofread and prompt — with output arriving as tracked changes you review.
  • Tracked changes and organisation. Review edits, work in chapters/scenes, use the binder, outliner, corkboard, metadata, and word counts/goals to manage your manuscript.
