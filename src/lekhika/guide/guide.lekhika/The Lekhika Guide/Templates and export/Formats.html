--- title: Formats kind: scene page_break: auto ---
  • Plain text — for pasting anywhere.
  • HTML — one file, with a stylesheet.
  • DOCX — for Word, and the basis of the PDF.
  • PDF — via LibreOffice, if it is installed.
  • EPUB — the ebook.

File → Print… (Ctrl+P) makes the same PDF and hands it to your system printer.
