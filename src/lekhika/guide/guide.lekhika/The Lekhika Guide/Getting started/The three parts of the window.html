--- title: The three parts of the window kind: scene page_break: auto ---

The binder is on the left: your project as a tree. Folders are containers that can hold more folders or documents (or other files, but they won't be visible in the software)

The page is in the middle: where you write. Depending on your selection in the binder, it can show an editor, page preview for documents and an outliner and corkboard view for folders. More on each type in its section later.

The inspector is on the right and offers further information about the selection depending on the kind of document or folder selected.
