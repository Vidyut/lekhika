--- title: Finding and replacing kind: scene page_break: auto ---

Ctrl+F searches inside the document you are in. Ctrl+Shift+F searches the whole project, and Ctrl+Shift+H searches and replaces across it.

The project-wide search lists every place the words appear, so you can jump from one to the next — useful when a character's name has to change in forty places, but replacing all is risky or when you want to know whether you have already said something.

Quick Open (Ctrl+Shift+P) jumps to any document by title. Standardise (Edit menu) normalises things like smart quotes or spacing.

Wiki links use [[Title]] to link to any section; hover to preview, click to jump.

The inspector can show Mentions / Mentioned by and Related files (documents that link to each other). The mentions scan can be toggled in Preferences → Links if it is distracting.
