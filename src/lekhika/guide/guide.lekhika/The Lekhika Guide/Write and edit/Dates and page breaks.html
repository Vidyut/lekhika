--- title: Dates and page breaks kind: scene page_break: auto ---

Edit → Insert Date puts the date at the time of writing at the caret; Insert Date and Time puts the date and the time. The format follows your system's locale.

Add something like not to be confused with $${currentDate/time} placeholder which will insert the date/time at the time of compiling. A plain $${Date} placeholder is deliberately left available for you to use in your metadata if you need it, and it can be whichever date you want - you could insert the current date in its value, or a book set in a different time period could use it for the in-world date value. These explanations are simply added here to avoid confusion with the inserted date from the editor, which is directly the date at the time of insertion as text.

Ctrl+Enter inserts a page break: the text after it starts a new page, and the page view shows it there.
