Paste is where Lekhika solves a common problem faced by writers who may need to copy-paste text back and forth for submitting for feedback or applying edits. Pasted text inserting the formatting like fonts, sizes and indents of the source text creates uneven formatting in the project, OR the opposite problem, where Ctrl+Shift+V strips all formatting and you lose your precious italics for emphasis, thoughts or other in-world elements.

This developer being an author with an active critiquing life herself, understands this. The default paste in Lekhika never inserts alien document formatting like fonts, indents, line spacing, sizes, etc, while retaining the character level emphasis, like italics, bold, or underline. So you can paste content into your document without worrying about the published document containing mangled formatting.

If your need to include/exclude formatting from the source changes, you can alter the behaviour of the default paste in preferences.

Paste comes in three flavours, chosen in Preferences:

  • Plain text — the words, none of the styling. Ctrl+Shift+V - unchanged.
  • Normal — bold, italics and underline, but not fonts and colours. Ctrl+V - our unique paste.
  • Keep all formatting — everything, as it came. (No shortcut; use Edit → Paste and Keep All Formatting.)

Plain text is the safe one when you are pasting from a web page that would otherwise bring its own fonts along.
