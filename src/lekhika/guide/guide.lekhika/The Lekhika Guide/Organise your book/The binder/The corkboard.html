--- title: The corkboard kind: scene page_break: auto ---

The corkboard is one card per document, laid out under the folder you selected. Each card shows the title, a synopsis preview and the word count, with element bands coloured from the item's inherited metadata.

It is for seeing a book as a set of pieces: which scenes are done, which are stubs, which two say the same thing. Use the header controls to collapse or expand all cards and to highlight cards by element; collapsed cards show the title and their element bands, and hovering a card shows more detail in a tooltip.

Click a card's title to open or close it. Click the card body to select it so its metadata can be edited in the inspector. Double-click a card to open the document in the editor.

The cards sit in the binder's order. To move one, drag it in the binder: the corkboard is a way of seeing the book, not a second place to rearrange it.
