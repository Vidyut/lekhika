--- title: The corkboard kind: scene page_break: auto ---

The corkboard is one card per document, laid out under the folder you selected. Each card carries the title, the synopsis, and the word count.

It is for seeing a book as a set of pieces: which scenes are done, which are stubs, which two say the same thing.

A single click on a card opens its synopsis, so you can write the one-line account of a scene without leaving the board. A double click opens the document itself.

The cards sit in the binder's order. To move one, drag it in the binder: the corkboard is a way of seeing the book, not a second place to rearrange it.
