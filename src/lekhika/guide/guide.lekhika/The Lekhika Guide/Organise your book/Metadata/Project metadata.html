With nothing selected — or with a folder selected — the inspector shows the whole-project fields: the Author, the synopsis of the book, and anything else you add. Every document can read them, and a document's own value wins over the project's.

This is where Author lives, which is why a title page can print it without every chapter having to carry it.

This is the place to add project-wide metadata: anything you add here becomes a placeholder available in every template, under its own name and as Metadata.<name>.
