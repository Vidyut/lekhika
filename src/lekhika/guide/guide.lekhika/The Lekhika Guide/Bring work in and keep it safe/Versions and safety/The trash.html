--- title: The trash kind: scene page_break: auto ---

Deleting a document moves it to the trash folder. The trash is a normal folder in the binder: you can look in it, drag something out of it, or empty it when you are sure.

Files in the trash keep a folder structure that mirrors where they came from. Restoring a file returns it to that original location by default; if you want it somewhere else, you can drag and drop it to the desired place in the binder.
