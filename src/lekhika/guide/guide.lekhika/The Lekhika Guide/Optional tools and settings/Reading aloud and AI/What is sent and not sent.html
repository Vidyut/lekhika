--- title: What is sent and not sent kind: scene page_break: auto ---

Lekhika does not phone home. There is no account, no telemetry and no upload. I have no idea who you are, what you are using the software for, or even whether you are using it.

The only outbound requests the app can make are the two you ask for: the AI endpoint you configured, and the update check in Preferences — which shows you the exact command to run and writes down what it did.

When you use an AI feature, the request you send includes the text you are working on. If you are concerned about AI services using unpublished work for training or other purposes, choose a service you trust or run a model locally so your requests do not leave your machine.
