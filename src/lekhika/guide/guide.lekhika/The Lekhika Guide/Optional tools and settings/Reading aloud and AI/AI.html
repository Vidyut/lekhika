AI → Continue Scene asks a model to carry on from where you stopped. AI → Proofread Selection looks at the text you have selected and proposes fixes — typos, grammar, doubled words, stray spaces — as the minimal edit, not a rewrite.

Both are off until you tick AI integration in Preferences and say where the model is:

  • A local model (Ollama) keeps everything on your machine. Nothing leaves the computer.
  • A hosted endpoint sends the text you ask about to that service, and nowhere else.

Whatever comes back arrives as tracked changes. You read them and accept or reject them one by one. Lekhika never applies an AI's words to your manuscript for you, and never quietly rewrites a paragraph because a model suggested it.

The proofreading prompt is deliberately narrow: bloopers, not prose. It is for the mistakes and leaves your voice alone.

AI → Prompt… asks the model anything; the answer comes back as a tracked insertion at the caret.

Set it up in Preferences: Detect local servers finds an Ollama on this machine, Add API… stores a hosted endpoint, and Install Ollama + phi4-mini… puts a small local model in place. AI is off by default — tick AI integration to enable it.
