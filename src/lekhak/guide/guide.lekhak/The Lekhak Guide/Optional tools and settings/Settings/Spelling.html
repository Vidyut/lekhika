The squiggles come from your system's dictionary, plus a word list of your own. Words you mark as correct are remembered, per project, in dictionary.txt — a plain file you can read and edit.

Preferences → Project scan scans your project for words Lekhak does not recognise and lets you review them in batches, so names and other project-specific terms can be added quickly.
