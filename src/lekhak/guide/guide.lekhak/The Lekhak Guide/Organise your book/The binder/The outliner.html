--- title: The outliner kind: scene page_break: auto ---

View → Outliner (or the tab beside the binder) shows your book as a table: title, type, word count, and whatever metadata columns you have asked for. It is the same book, read as a list — useful when you want to see the shape of a folder's documents rather than its sentences.

Document → Outliner Columns… chooses the columns. The list is taken from your own project: the four the table always has, and every metadata field your documents actually carry, so what you can add is what your book has rather than a fixed set. A column shows the value a document has, including one it inherits from the folder above it.

Select more than one row and Bulk Edit… appears above the table: change the Type or the metadata of everything selected at once, which is the quick way to mark a run of documents as done, or to give them all the same point of view.

Expand all / Collapse all in the outliner opens or closes the whole tree at once. Quick Open (Ctrl+Shift+P) jumps to any document by title across the project.
